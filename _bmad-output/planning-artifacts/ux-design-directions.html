Direction 3: Slite Gradient

Direction 5: Dark Immersive
Dark-first design — near-black background (#0a0a0f), purple-violet accent (#a78bfa), subtle transparency layers, minimal borders using rgba. Feels like coding at midnight. Immersive, focused, modern. The default experience for developers and night owls. Light mode would be the alternate, not the primary.

Direction 6: Editorial Hybrid ★ RECOMMENDED
The synthesis — near-white backgrounds (#fefefe) with barely-there borders (#f0f0f0), editor as a distinct column with subtle side borders (like a page), squared notebook color dots (3px radius), generous line-height (1.75), purple accent used sparingly (#7B68EE). Combines Ugmonk's intentionality, hm.la's confidence, Slite's friendliness, and Prometheus's clarity. Feels like quality stationery — simple, considered, and unmistakably itself.
